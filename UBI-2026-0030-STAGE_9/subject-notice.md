# Subject Notice

This notice covers direct communication to affected individuals under two separate obligations: GDPR Article 34 for EU high-risk individuals and California Civil Code 1798.82 for California consumers.

Each section is based on its own ledger entry, so the deadlines and affected populations are different.

## EU high-risk individuals: GDPR Article 34

**Status:** Triggered. There is no fixed statutory deadline; notification must be made without undue delay.

**What we know:** Your data may have included information beyond basic contact details. 620 individuals have been confirmed as affected. A further 85 individuals are still under investigation while the backup index is being reconciled. These individuals have not yet been confirmed, giving an upper bound of 705.

**What this means:** If your record falls within the higher-risk subset, the exposed information may include category-specific information in addition to your contact details. You are being notified directly because of this elevated risk.

**What we are doing:** Containment of the incident has been completed. Further remediation steps are being finalized.

**Contact:** [dpo@example.invalid](mailto:dpo@example.invalid) (Data Protection Officer)

## California consumers: Civil Code 1798.82

**Status:** Triggered.
**Deadline:** 12 August 2026 at 07:35 UTC (08:35 WAT), 30 calendar days from 13 July 2026 at 07:35 UTC.

**What happened:** An unauthorized actor accessed data that included your account email and password hash and, for some individuals, medical information. The actor has not been identified.

**530 individuals have been confirmed as affected.**

**What we know and what is still being investigated:** The exposure of account credentials, including email addresses and password hashes, has been confirmed. The extent of the medical-information exposure depends on the encryption-key assessment for this case, which is `encryption_key_acquired=True`. This information may be updated as the investigation continues.

**What we are doing:** Containment has been completed. We recommend monitoring your accounts while we finalize additional remediation steps.

No law-enforcement delay has been requested for this notice.

**Contact:** [dpo@example.invalid](Data Protection Officer)
